Call for Speakers and Sponsors

征集演讲嘉宾与赞助伙伴

The 2027 symposium will explore how disease research informs therapeutic development. IBA plans 10 sessions, including a keynote, with more than 50 speakers.

本届研讨会将探讨如何将疾病研究成果转化为新疗法。会议计划设置包括主旨演讲在内的 10 个专场，拟邀请超过 50 位演讲嘉宾。

Building on the success of the 2026 symposium, the 2027 program will feature parallel sessions, allowing more time for presentations, in-depth scientific discussion, and networking.

在 2026 年研讨会成功举办的基础上，2027 年将设置平行分会场，为专题报告、深入的学术讨论和交流联络提供更充裕的时间。

To propose a talk or ask about sponsoring the symposium, email admin@iba-usa.org.

如有意演讲或赞助本次研讨会，请将演讲题目或赞助意向发送至 admin@iba-usa.org。
