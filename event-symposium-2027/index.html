2027 IBA Scientific Symposium

2027 IBA 科学研讨会

Advancing Translational Science: Disease Research and Therapeutic Development

推进转化科学：疾病研究与治疗创新

Building on the success of the 2026 IBA Annual Conference and our previous spring symposium, the Innovative Biopharmaceutical Association (IBA) is excited to announce an expanded 2027 Scientific Symposium, bringing together university professors, leading academic researchers, and biopharmaceutical industry experts to share the latest scientific discoveries, emerging technologies, and advances in therapeutic development.

在 2026 IBA 年会及上一届春季研讨会成功举办的基础上，创新生物医药协会（IBA）欣然宣布，2027 科学研讨会将进一步扩大规模，汇聚大学教授、杰出学术研究人员与生物医药行业专家，分享最新科学发现、新兴技术及治疗方法研发进展。

With a strong emphasis on academic research and scientific innovation, the symposium provides a valuable platform for professors and researchers to showcase their latest discoveries, exchange ideas with peers, and explore opportunities for collaboration with industry leaders.

研讨会将重点关注学术研究与科学创新，为教授和科研人员提供展示最新发现、与同行交流观点，以及探索与行业领袖合作机会的平台。

What's New in 2027?

2027 年有哪些新亮点？

This year's symposium will feature 10 scientific sessions and over 50 planned speakers from academia and industry.

本届研讨会将设置10 个科学专题专场，计划邀请超过 50 位来自学术界和产业界的演讲嘉宾。

Unlike our previous spring symposium, the 2027 event will feature multiple parallel sessions, allowing more professors, researchers, and industry experts to present their work while providing longer presentation times and greater opportunities for in-depth scientific discussions.

与上一届春季研讨会相比，2027 年活动将设置多个平行分会场，让更多教授、科研人员和行业专家有机会介绍自己的工作，并提供更充裕的报告时间与更多深入开展学术讨论的机会。

The program will highlight advances in disease research, translational science, innovative therapies, and drug development, along with discussions on research funding, entrepreneurship, and career development.

议程将重点介绍疾病研究、转化科学、创新疗法与药物研发的进展，并围绕科研资助、创业及职业发展展开讨论。

Call for Speakers & Sponsors

征集演讲嘉宾与赞助伙伴

Call for Speakers: We warmly invite university professors, academic researchers, and industry leaders to present their latest scientific discoveries, innovative research, and advances in therapeutic development. We particularly encourage professors to take advantage of this opportunity to share their research with a broad scientific audience and connect with potential collaborators.

征集演讲嘉宾：我们诚邀大学教授、学术研究人员和行业领袖分享最新科学发现、创新研究及治疗方法研发进展。特别欢迎教授们借此机会，向广泛的科研同仁介绍自己的研究，并与潜在合作伙伴建立联系。

Call for Sponsors: We welcome pharmaceutical companies, biotechnology firms, CROs, and other organizations interested in supporting scientific research, promoting innovation, and strengthening academia-industry partnerships.

征集赞助伙伴：欢迎制药企业、生物技术公司、合同研究组织（CRO）以及其他有意支持科学研究、推动创新、加强学术界与产业界合作的机构参与赞助。

Our goal is not simply to expand the symposium, but to create more opportunities for scientific discovery, meaningful exchange, and collaboration between academia and industry.

我们的目标不仅是扩大研讨会规模，更是为科学发现、深入交流以及学术界与产业界的合作创造更多机会。

For speaking and sponsorship opportunities, please contact admin@iba-usa.org.

如希望了解演讲与赞助机会，请联系 admin@iba-usa.org。

We look forward to welcoming you to the 2027 IBA Scientific Symposium!

期待在2027 IBA 科学研讨会与您相聚！
