Dear IBA members and friends, summer is here, and one of IBA’s most anticipated community events is back.

亲爱的 IBA 会员及朋友们：盛夏已至，又到了我们一年一度相聚的时刻！

The Innovative Biopharmaceutical Association warmly invites you, your family, friends, and colleagues to join us for the 2026 IBA BBQ & Music Festival at Norristown Farm Park.

创新生物医药协会（IBA）诚挚邀请您和您的家人、朋友及同事参加 2026 IBA BBQ & Music Festival（IBA 夏日烧烤 & 音乐节）！

Last year, nearly 600 friends and families joined us for a summer gathering none of us has forgotten: the food, the music, the laughter, and the chance to see old friends and meet new ones. IBA was founded in 2025 by the core leadership team of the former CBA-GP. We are making this year even bigger and better: alongside the classic barbecue and summer concert, expect family activities, a lucky draw, face-to-face career conversations, board games, and plenty more.

还记得去年近 600 位朋友和家人共同参与的 BBQ 盛会吗？美食、音乐、欢笑，以及新朋老友相聚的热闹场景，至今令人难忘。IBA 由原 CBA-GP 管理团队核心班底于 2025 年创立。我们将在延续经典的基础上全面升级！不仅有丰富的 BBQ 美食和精彩的夏韵音乐会，还有亲子互动、幸运抽奖、职场面对面、桌游交友，以及更多轻松有趣的互动环节。
