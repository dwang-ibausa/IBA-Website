About the Symposium

研讨会简介

The Scientific Symposium on Translational Biomedicine is the association's main academic meeting of the year. Researchers and biopharmaceutical scientists from across the region come together for a day of presentations, panel discussions, and conversation. The agenda, the speakers, and the session topics are all set out in the booklet below.

转化医学科学研讨会是协会一年中最重要的学术会议。本地区的高校研究人员与生物医药科学家将齐聚一堂，参加为期一天的主题报告、圆桌讨论与交流。会议日程、演讲嘉宾与专题安排，详见下方的会议手册。

We welcome proposals for talks and posters, as well as sponsorship inquiries, at admin@iba-usa.org.
