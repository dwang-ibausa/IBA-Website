Membership

会员

Membership is open to anyone working or studying in the biopharmaceutical sciences.

凡在生物医药领域工作或学习的人士，均可申请加入本协会。

Why Join

加入的理由

Members pay considerably less to attend IBA events.

会员参加协会各项活动的费用大幅降低。

Membership dues also pay for the association's scientific program, and they are part of what keeps ticket prices within reach of students and early-career scientists.

会费同时用于支持协会的学术活动，也正因如此，我们才能将票价维持在学生与青年科研人员可以负担的水平。

The association is run by volunteers from the regional biopharmaceutical community. Joining is how most members come to take part: speaking at a session, helping the organizing committee, or bringing a colleague into the group.

协会由本地区生物医药界的志愿者共同运营。多数会员正是通过加入协会而参与其中：在会议上发言、协助组委会工作，或将同事引荐给这个群体。

How to Join

如何加入

  1. Open the membership page using the button below, or scan the code with your phone camera.
  2. 点击下方按钮打开会员申请页面，或使用手机相机扫描二维码。
  3. Choose the student and postdoc rate or the professional rate.
  4. 选择学生与博士后档或在职专业人士档。
  5. Complete payment. Membership runs for one year from the date you join.
  6. 完成付款。会员资格自加入之日起一年内有效。
  7. Register for events separately. Your member rate is applied at checkout.
  8. 各项活动需另行报名，结算时将自动适用会员价。
