Who we are

协会简介

IBA-USA is a nonprofit organization home-based in Greater Philadelphia and dedicated to advancing the communication, translation, and commercialization of cutting-edge scientific discoveries and pharmaceutical R&D innovations.

IBA-USA 是一家总部位于大费城地区的非营利组织，致力于推动前沿科学发现与生物医药研发创新的交流、转化与产业化。

We primarily serve U.S.-based academic investigators and biopharmaceutical companies, with a mission to build a high-level national platform that fosters drug development through the active engagement of scientists, clinical experts, entrepreneurs, and venture capital partners across the United States.

我们主要服务于美国本土的学术研究者与生物医药企业，致力于打造一个高水平的全国性平台，通过科学家、临床专家、创业者与风险投资伙伴的深度参与，共同促进新药研发。

How IBA-USA serves its members

协会为会员提供的支持

Communication

交流

We convene symposia and seminars at which academic investigators and industry scientists present their work to one another, and where the questions raised carry on well past the session.

我们举办研讨会与专题讲座，让高校研究人员与产业界科学家互相报告各自的工作，会后的讨论往往同样有价值。

Translation

转化

Our scientific program concentrates on translational medicine: the passage from a laboratory finding to a first study in patients, where the majority of promising candidates are lost.

我们的学术活动聚焦转化医学，即从实验室发现走向首次人体研究的阶段，大多数看似有希望的候选药物正是在这一环节失败。

Commercialization

产业化

We introduce members to entrepreneurs and investors, because a sound scientific case still requires capital and an organization willing to carry it through development.

我们为会员引荐创业者与投资人，因为科学上站得住的项目，仍需要资金以及愿意将其推进到开发阶段的企业。
