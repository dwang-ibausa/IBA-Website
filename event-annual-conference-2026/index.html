About the Annual Conference

关于年会

The IBA Annual Conference is the association’s premier industry-focused event, bringing together biopharmaceutical executives, industry leaders, scientists, entrepreneurs, and investors to exchange insights, explore emerging innovations, and foster strategic collaborations across the drug development ecosystem.

IBA 年会是协会面向生物医药行业的年度重要活动，汇聚生物医药企业高管、行业领袖、科学家、创业者与投资人，交流见解、探索新兴创新成果，并推动药物研发生态中各方的战略合作。

The theme of the 2026 Annual Conference, “Converging Innovations: AI, Next-Generation Biomedical Science, and Global Drug Development,” reflects the rapidly evolving biopharmaceutical landscape and the growing importance of collaboration across science, technology, and industry.

2026 年会以“创新汇聚：人工智能、新一代生物医学科学与全球药物研发”为主题，体现了生物医药行业的快速发展，以及跨科学、技术与产业协作日益重要的作用。

The full-day program featured 10 sessions covering artificial intelligence and real-world evidence, oncology, neurological diseases, cardiovascular and metabolic diseases, new approach methodologies (NAMs) and organoids, statistics and regulatory strategy, CMC challenges, entrepreneurship and business development, and career development.

全天议程设置了10 个环节，涵盖人工智能与真实世界证据、肿瘤、神经系统疾病、心血管与代谢疾病、新方法学（NAMs）与类器官专题讨论、统计与法规策略、CMC 挑战、创业与商务拓展专题专题，以及职业发展。

The final attendee affiliation list represents more than 100 companies and business organizations and includes more than 60 executives and senior leaders, alongside 11 sponsoring organizations. This industry participation provided opportunities to connect with decision-makers, exchange perspectives, explore business opportunities, and build strategic partnerships.

最终参会者所属机构名单涵盖100 多家企业及商业机构，并包含60 余位高管及高级领导者，同时有11 家赞助机构支持。广泛的行业参与为参会者提供了与决策者建立联系、交流观点、探索商业机会和建立战略伙伴关系的宝贵机会。

The conference concluded with an Evening Keynote, Networking Dinner, and Live Entertainment, offering attendees a relaxed and enjoyable setting to continue conversations, strengthen relationships, and celebrate the spirit of innovation and collaboration.

年会以晚间主旨演讲、交流晚宴与现场文艺表演收尾，为参会者提供轻松愉快的氛围，继续交流、增进联系，并共同庆祝创新与合作的精神。

Thank you for a day of innovation, leadership, and meaningful connections, bringing together the people and ideas shaping the future of biopharmaceutical development.

感谢大家共同度过汇聚创新、领导力与深度交流的一天，连接共同塑造生物医药研发未来的人才与思想。
